## 2. Ad-break plan
Principles (from the retention skill and the script's structure): breaks go right after a chapter-end hook, never inside a scene; a viewer waiting on an answer sits through an ad; no break before the story has earned the viewer; no break inside or immediately after the title-question payoff; none in the last ~5 minutes.

**Recommended: three manual mid-rolls at chapter changes.** Times are estimates (chapter start from `word_count.json`); in Studio, place them in the silence after the preceding line's `{p=}` pause once audio exists.

Spacing: 8:50, 8:28, 6:09. Last ad to the end: 10:34.

**Zones to keep ad-free:** 0:00-8:50 (hook + establishing), 17:18-23:27 (the three payoffs: who fired, the oath, the uncle), and ≈28:00 to the end (climax, second oath, "I love you", ending). Not recommended: breaks at 19:21 or 20:14 (between the who-fired payoff and the oath payoff) because they would sit on the film's emotional peak.

**Lighter alternative (one break):** only #2 at ≈17:18, if the owner prefers fewer interruptions on a first video from a new channel. I have no data to say which earns more or loses more viewers; that needs the channel's own retention graph.

**Fallback if the real runtime runs long or short:** each break is tied to a chapter boundary, not a clock time. Keep the chapters, move the minute marks. Do not let YouTube auto-place mid-rolls: auto placement can land inside a scene. Whether a channel can disable auto placement and set manual breaks depends on its monetization status, which I could not verify.
