## 2. The 4:10 shooting - who heard, who saw, who fired
- **Ivy heard** two shots "so close together they sounded like one word", from behind her car [p.10, 57]. She counted to nine, looked, and **saw the aftermath**: a man sliding down a silver sedan, a second gun a foot from his open hand, Nico standing over him with a gun in his **left** hand, his right arm in her white splint, and a jacket going through the stairwell door [p.10].
- **Ivy never saw who fired.** She says so under oath on Nov 13: "I didn't see who fired" / she saw "what was in his hands afterward" [p.327]. Early on she only tells the FBI what she saw (Nico, left-hand gun) [p.17, 168].
- **Who fired: Enzo Vescari (21).** Enzo follows Frankie, waits half a flight up the stairs, a shoe scrapes, Frankie turns with his gun rising; Enzo fires twice [p.146-147, 314]. Nico, on level two, takes the gun from Enzo's hand with his **left** hand (right is in the splint) and tells him to go [p.147, 320, 323]. The gun was a gift from Sal [p.314]. Nico buries it in the Van Brunt St planter [p.323]; the FBI digs it up [p.328].
- **Frankie** was hired by Sal ($20,000, ledger entry "F.L. 20. Paid.", Oct 1) to shoot Nico, lost his nerve, and says "somebody in your house paid me" [p.97, 228, 254].
- **Paulie Ferraro** (Brancato's kitchen mole) held the level-three stairwell door and sent Enzo down ("Frankie's waiting"), then watched from the top of the ramp [p.195, 235, 273].
- **Design rule for the film:** the injured right hand is **never** offered as proof Nico could not shoot left-handed. Ivy only ever claims what she heard and saw; the shooter is established by Enzo's confession, Nico's account, the buried gun, and Frankie's armed hand. (Ivy herself says she "knew" Nico couldn't have fired [p.147]; the film leaves that inference out.)

## 4. Motives
- **Sal:** love and fear of losing the family; sold the port through Brancato; paid for Frankie to kill Nico; ran the pill supply through Bell and a gray Lincoln; told Brancato about Bell's deadline; tried to frame Theo. "Somebody in this family had to be his father. You were busy being a saint." [p.253]
- **Brancato:** wants the port; leverage over Bell (a dead cousin); sends Paulie and Lenny; son addicted to the same pills [p.267-269, 297].
- **Bell:** son Danny's $470,000 treatment; sells Ivy's schedule; signs the diversion form; writes a confession letter and is murdered before delivering it [p.127, 241-242].
- **Nico:** protects Enzo (took the gun), will not touch civilians, chose St. Brigid's partly because of the doctor who sat with Rosaria Amato [p.96-97, 335].
- **Enzo:** guilt over the 86th St hit-and-run (a boy named Anthony lost a leg) and over Frankie [p.233-234, 313-314].
- **Ivy:** will not lie under oath; will not hand over a 21-year-old; chooses to waive privilege herself [p.326-328].

- **Ivy:** 28, freckles, red messy bun **with a pen**, navy scrubs at work, her late mother's gray cardigan elsewhere [p.5, 46, 231]. **Ring:** Nonna Rosa's square emerald (too big) goes on at the wedding [p.49]; at work it hangs on a **thin gold chain under her scrubs** (not a pendant) [p.61]; on her finger at court and home [p.82, 162]; from Nov 5 a plain gold band under it stops it turning [p.261-262].
- **Nico:** 34, black/dark **wavy** hair and stubble (reference art), gray eyes, thin white scar through the **left** eyebrow [p.6]. Right-forearm splint: **white plaster Oct 3-10, black thermoplastic with 3 velcro straps from Oct 10** [p.106], Teresa's small white cross on the wrist strap [p.236], removed Thu Nov 13 [p.329]. Drives, writes, holds the gun and kisses her wrist with his **left** hand.
- **Enzo:** gold curls, gray jacket, 21. **Sal:** silver hair, camel coat, anise candies in white twists, Monteleone's cannoli. **Lucia:** black dress, gold cross, hand to the cross when afraid. **Reyes:** rain jacket in any weather. **Holloway:** navy suit, straightens his cuffs. **Brancato:** brown cardigan, buttons wrong.
- **Garage:** St. Brigid's, Red Hook, level 2, spot 2-14, orange lights, wet concrete, camera dome dead since Tuesday [p.10, 14].

## 7. What the adaptation compressed, cut, merged, or invented (audit)
- **Cut:** Kevin (ex); most of Aldo's dinner and Bianca's gala cruelty; Pete Cusumano frame plan; Manny/Kessler/Teresa-sheets testimony; the review board; Helen's aortic dissection; mock cross; Vince Amoroso (merged into the Paulie identification); detention hearings; Aldo/Bianca "promised" subplot.
- **Merged / reordered:** Nico finding the Lincoln (Ch.24) is told as a recollection inside the glove-box chapter; the Bell letter (Ch.33) opens the confrontation chapter.
- **Composed, not verbatim canon:** the Nico-POV garage flashback (CH09) blends p.97-98 (Frankie's call, the scrape, the gun rising) with Enzo's and Nico's later accounts [p.146-147, 314, 320, 323]; the novel withholds Nico's POV of the shots themselves. Several short bridges and reflections ("I have thought about that yes every day since") are adaptation additions. All visual descriptions are the adaptation's.
- **Not changed:** who fired, who paid, the pills' chain, Sal's exile, Ivy's two oaths, Nico's gun confession, the divorce refused, "She does." / "Term six.", the HEA, the closed-door handling of intimacy.
